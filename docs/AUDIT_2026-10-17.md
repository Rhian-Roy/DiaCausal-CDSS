# Audit of 17 Oct 2026: what was checked, what was fixed, what is still open

> Research prototype for clinician evaluation; not a marketed medical device; not for unsupervised clinical use.

**Question asked:** is the whole project working properly, with nothing missed and nothing faulty?
**Short answer:** it works and every automated check passes, but the audit found **one real safety bug** (now fixed) and several
smaller gaps. It could **not** be a complete audit: the plan was to use 14 parallel auditors, and the usage limit stopped most of
them. This page says exactly what was and was not covered.

## 2. Found and fixed in the pull request of this audit

Each was **reproduced by hand** before being fixed, and each now has a test that fails on the old code.

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **critical** | The emergency check was skipped when the message also held a "general question" word (risk, versus, compared, likely, ...). "My patient is unconscious, which add-on carries less risk?" got through, in Python and in the browser | A message that points at one patient (my patient, this patient, he, she, patient is, ...) is never read as a general drug question. | 2 | high | The output checks for a model's draft ignored the short name "SU" (the word the model is shown) and the sulfonylurea molecules of the corpus (gliclazide, glibenclamide, glipizide): "SU is a good choice" passed although SU was excluded | added to `diacausal/guards/output_guards.yaml` |
| 3 | high | A dose written in words or grams ("two tablets twice a day", "2 g", "every 12 hours") passed output check 4 | patterns added to `output_guards.yaml` |

## 3. Reported by the auditors, **not yet fixed** (none was independently re-verified)

The two auditors that finished found these. They are real observations with evidence, but the review step that tries to refute each
one did not run, so treat them as "probably true, decide what to do":

| Severity | Finding | Suggested action |
|---|---|---|
| high | The language-model benchmark numbers that fill version D of the A–D table (number match 35 %, fallback 70 %) were measured on 10 Oct, **before** the drivers were added to the prompt (P25) and before the number check was extended (P26) | re-run `python scripts/bench_llm.py --all` with Ollama (about 20 minutes), then `python scripts/xai_ablation.py` and the website export; or label the D writing rows "measured before P25" everywhere they appear |
| medium | A and B are compared on different sets of options: A may pick among every option the rules leave, B only among those it did not abstain on; `best_pick_rate_same_patients` from `docs/XAI_PLAN.md` is not computed | compute it, or say in the report that regret is not like-for-like |
| medium | Driver keywords can change whether retrieval abstains, so a card can say "no passage answers this question" and hide every estimate | decide with Member B whether drivers may only reorder |
| medium | The scope and identifier checks are word lists: a name without a title ("Ramesh Kumar has HbA1c 8.4"), a phone number written in words, "He is 16" (age without "years old") or lower-case PAN pass | an inherent limit of deterministic guards (the plan says they catch the obvious wordings); document it in the report |

| low | The browser and Python guards disagree on fullwidth Latin letters | rare; align `_is_latin` |

## 4. What this audit did **not** cover

The usage limit stopped 12 of the 14 planned auditors twice. They never reported. Covered only by the baseline and the checks in
section 1, not by an auditor: the plan, prompt by prompt, for P00–P20 and P27–P29; the website in a real browser beyond the existing
screenshot and phone tests; a read of every doc for accuracy beyond the path check; a deep review of `supabase/migrations`; the clinic
laptop beyond the P29b session; CI beyond the status of the last run.

**Still unverified for everyone:** the Windows start script (no Windows machine), a full run with Wi-Fi off, the RTX 2050 latency, the
model table in `docs/OFFLINE_INSTALL.md`, and everything that depends on teammates and the doctor (see `docs/STATUS_2026-10-11.md`).
