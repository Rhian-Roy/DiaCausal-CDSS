Both charts follow the department's Project Interaction Sheet (Second Half of 2026 and First Half of 2027). Figure~\ref{fig:timeline} lists every entry of the sheet with its date and status; weeks 0--7 are signed by the guide. Figure~\ref{fig:gantt} shows the same plan as a Gantt chart. As the sheet requires, implementation is split into four sections: (1) the causal engine core and the chat application base, (2) the evidence search (RAG), secondary outcomes and the website, (3) the causal engine and RAG inside the chat application, and (4) better retrieval, a real-data adapter and the evaluation tools. The charts contain only the sheet's own rows and wording. The sheet's implementation checks are 25\% by 23 September (signed), 40\% by 7 October (met on 28 September), 60\% by 14 October (met on 29 September) and 80\% by 21 October (team target 3 October); the team's target for all four sections is 6 October. Diamonds mark Synopsis Presentations I (12 August) and II (30 September 2026); the dashed line marks today.
